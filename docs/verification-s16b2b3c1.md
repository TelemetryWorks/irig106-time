## Policy and bounds

Coordinator decisions, not IRIG-mandated reset flags: a >30s backward step
resets immediately; exactly 30s does not. Otherwise retain at most two pending
late references per channel. A third strictly increasing reference still behind
the baseline confirms reset at the FIRST packet. Non-increasing runs break;
one/two pending points at EOF remain out-of-order. Findings are generated at
classification time, with original packet indices, not retrospectively removed
from sampled counts. Other-channel packets do not break a per-channel run.

Reset increments the channel segment, clears its correlation and known-query
candidates, and restarts its pair baseline. Replaying the confirmed run checks
only new-segment pairs; reference indices retain original arrival order. Keep
valid gap extrema across segments, never the boundary gap. Other channel state
is retained. clear_channel is a caller-directed resource operation, not library
reset inference: it frees channel storage, counts removals and recomputes the
cache's latest retained RTC. Library no_std/alloc and no-I/O boundary stay.

Memory: at most two pending points/channel (a third is transient), existing
1,024-reference global cache, 20 finding/jump samples and fixed per-channel
inventory. No archive of reset segments is retained yet. No dependencies,
unsafe, settings, package publication or local large-file RSS work.

reports needs-context WARNING and no silent absolute pick; live pending/reset
answers also remain counter-only. These guards keep provisional/old/new
